# Popular duplicate photo/video cleaners (Android)

From general knowledge, not verified against current store listings. Check ratings and pricing in Google Play before relying on any.

| App | Notes |
|---|---|
| Google Files (Files by Google) | Free, ad-light. "Clean" tab finds duplicate files, junk, large files. Exact duplicates mainly. |
| Google Photos | Not a dedupe tool, but "Free up space" removes backed-up local copies. Use as the safety net. |
| Remo Duplicate Photos Remover | Dedicated photo duplicate and similar-photo finder. |
| Duplicate Photos Fixer Pro (Systweak) | Finds exact and similar photos, auto-marks. Free with ads and a paid tier. |
| Duplicate Files Fixer (Systweak) | Exact file duplicates including videos and audio. |
| Gemini Photos | Smart similar-photo grouping with best-shot suggestion; mostly iOS, check Android availability. |
| Photo Cleaner / Cleaner Guru / CCleaner | General cleaners with duplicate and similar-photo tools; ad and upsell heavy. |
| SD Maid (SD Maid SE) | Power-user cleanup incl. duplicate finder; strong on app junk, less on photo similarity. |
| Duplicate Cleaner / Search Duplicate File | Simple hash-based exact duplicate finders. |
| Samsung Gallery / Device Care | On Samsung phones, built-in duplicate and similar image suggestions. |

Takeaways for this project: the best apps group similar shots and pre-select a best copy for review. Exact-hash tools miss near-duplicates, which is the gap M3 to M5 target. Common complaints: aggressive ads, auto-delete without clear review, no undo. Our design avoids those with review-first and a trash with undo.
